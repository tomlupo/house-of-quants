# Research

Longer-form quantitative research and strategy studies.

Each publication should aim to answer a clear question, explain the methodology, show the relevant evidence, and separate robust conclusions from assumptions or open questions.

## Research template

A typical note can follow this structure:

1. Research question
2. Motivation
3. Data and universe
4. Methodology
5. Results
6. Robustness checks
7. Implementation considerations
8. Conclusions
9. Selected code or notebook, where useful

Research will be added here over time.
