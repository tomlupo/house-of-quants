# House of Quants

## Quantitative Research & Solutions

House of Quants is an independent research publication focused on systematic investing, portfolio construction, quantitative methods, research infrastructure, and AI-enabled analytical workflows.

The focus is on practical, reproducible work: clear research questions, transparent methodology, robust testing, and implementation-aware conclusions.

### What you will find here

- **Research** — longer studies and strategy research
- **Notes** — shorter observations, methods, and ideas
- **Code** — selected examples, notebooks, and reusable tools

Most production systems and proprietary research remain private. Public material is intended to show the reasoning, methods, and selected implementation details behind the work.
